# GoldPsychR Expansion: Executive Summary

**Date:** August 8, 2026  
**Status:** Planning Phase Complete

---

## Current State

**GoldPsychR** is a well-designed 4-session R statistics course for psychology students at Goldsmiths:

- **Structure:** 4 × 2-hour labs covering descriptives, t-tests, ANOVA, and regression
- **Pedagogy:** Three-stage learning (slides → WebR practice → RStudio exercises)
- **Platform:** Quarto website with WebR integration
- **Quality:** Professional design, good pedagogical scaffolding, real psychology data
- **Resources:** Includes "Boil in the Bag" templates and R Toolbox (partially complete)

---

## Proposed Extension

Transform into a **comprehensive 12-16 week programme** covering the full research workflow:

### Core 12-Week Programme

| Weeks | Focus | Content |
|-------|-------|---------|
| 1-3 | **Foundations** | R basics, data wrangling, visualization fundamentals (NEW) |
| 4-7 | **Inference** | Descriptives, probability, t-tests, ANOVA (EXPANDED from current Labs 1-3) |
| 8 | **Mid-Term** | Integrative project (NEW) |
| 9-10 | **Regression** | Correlation, simple & multiple regression (EXPANDED from current Lab 4) |
| 11 | **Communication** | Publication figures, Quarto reporting (NEW) |
| 12 | **Capstone** | Independent analysis project (NEW) |

### Optional Extended Programme (Weeks 13-16)

- Week 13: Repeated measures & mixed designs
- Week 14: Logistic regression  
- Week 15-16: Advanced topics (moderation/mediation, mixed models, or open science)

---

## What Gets Added

### New Content Modules (6 new weeks)
1. **Week 1: R Foundations** - RStudio, R basics (currently assumed knowledge)
2. **Week 2: Data Wrangling** - tidyverse, dplyr, data cleaning (scattered currently)
3. **Week 3: Data Visualization I** - ggplot2 comprehensive (introduced but not taught)
4. **Week 5: Probability & Sampling** - Statistical foundations (missing)
5. **Week 11: Visualization II & Reporting** - Publication workflow (partial)
6. **Week 8 & 12: Projects** - Integrative assessments (NEW)

### Expanded Existing Content
- Lab 1 → Week 4: Add distributions, outliers, transformations
- Lab 2 → Week 6: Add hypothesis testing theory, p-value deep dive, non-parametric tests
- Lab 3 → Week 7: Add assumptions checking, planned contrasts, complex designs
- Lab 4 → Weeks 9-10: Split into two weeks, add diagnostics, model comparison

### Enhanced Resources
- **Complete R Toolbox** (expand 8 placeholder sections + add 4 new sections)
- **Expand Boil in the Bag** (add 6 new templates)
- **Example Reports** (create 4 model research reports)
- **Cheat Sheets** (create 5 psychology-focused reference cards)
- **Video Content** (6 priority screencasts)
- **Instructor Materials** (teaching notes, rubrics, solutions)
